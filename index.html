A small, unserious thing ❤️

Somewhere along the way, you became my favourite person to be completely unserious with.

I don't know when exactly it happened—somewhere between yello and blue, your increasingly exhausted eye rolls, and my apparently incurable need to ruffle your feathers just enough to make you laugh. You call me annoying, I insist it is a privilege reserved for very few people, and somehow we keep returning to the same ridiculous conversations as though neither of us has anything better to do.

Maybe that's the point.

With you, even doing absolutely nothing feels like something.

Then there are the ordinary things. The random pictures, the funny cars, the “look at this” messages, the calls that begin nowhere and somehow become two hours long. A good lunch. Something my boss said. A song that suddenly sounds like you.

Somehow, you're the first person I want to tell.

And then there is the shilling.

I think I owe that little coin an apology.

It was never meant to become important. Just the smallest difference between forty-nine and fifty, barely enough to notice. And yet somehow it became a joke, then a little argument, then a ridiculous excuse to tell you that you're the one I want.

• • •

Funny how these things happen with you.

Nothing announces itself.

A missing shilling becomes a reason to smile. Githeri becomes an invitation. A two-hour conversation begins with nothing and ends with me wondering how the night disappeared so quickly.

Perhaps that's what you do to ordinary things.
You make them worth remembering.

I think the hoodie has been getting ideas too.

It started innocently enough—a joke about closet space, one ridiculous piece of clothing pretending it had already earned residency. Now it sits there, imaginary and entirely too confident, as though it knows something I don't.

Perhaps it does.

Because there is something strange about imagining a little piece of myself among your things. Not because it has to mean anything yet, but because for a moment I can picture the ordinary version of us—mornings, misplaced things, food shared without keeping score, you telling me I'm annoying while making space for me anyway.

• • •

And maybe that's why I laughed when you said three months.

You were talking about closet space... I thought hoodie.

I was already wondering how much room there might be for a life.

And perhaps that's what I like most.

Not the grand things, but the quiet ones;the laughter that lingers after a call, the ridiculous arguments over nothing, the githeri, the missing shilling, the imaginary space for a hoodie.

The way happiness with you doesn't arrive making an entrance.
It simply sits beside me and feels like home.

I don't know exactly what tomorrow makes of any of this. And for once, I don't think I need to.

I just know that somewhere between the teasing and the tenderness, I found something I don't want to edit, hurry, or overthink.

I rather like being here with you.

Ready for the important part?
